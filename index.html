Getting Started

How to Setup LivingRoom IPTV

Follow these quick steps to start streaming in minutes.

1

Launch App on Apple TV

Open LivingRoom IPTV from your Apple TV Home screen. On first launch, you will see the Setup Gate screen.

2

Select Your Language

Click the Language button in the top-right corner to choose your preferred interface language (English, Turkish, German, French, Italian, Spanish).

3

Enter Your Provider Credentials or Try Demo

Fill in your Xtream Codes API Server URL (e.g. http://example.com:8080), Username, and Password, then click Connect & Verify.

Don't have a provider yet? Click Demo Modu / Demo Mode to immediately test the player with legal open-source test streams!

4

Enjoy 4K Streaming

Browse Live TV channels, Movies, and TV Series using the Apple TV Remote trackpad or directional buttons with instant playback.
