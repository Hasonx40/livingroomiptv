Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

1. Summary: Zero Data Collection

LivingRoom IPTV does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers. We strongly believe your entertainment viewing habits are entirely private and belong only to you.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Apple TV

All configuration data required for the app to function is saved strictly within your Apple TV's local application sandbox (via UserDefaults and local cache):

  • Your Xtream Codes API server URL, username, and password.
  • Your favorite channels list.
  • Your interface language preference (e.g. English, Turkish).
  • Live TV recordings created via the in-app record button.

This data is never sent to our servers, third parties, or cloud databases. When you uninstall the application from your Apple TV, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking SDKs

LivingRoom IPTV contains zero third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or ad networks). The application does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Media Playback & Network Requests

When streaming television channels, movies, or series, direct HTTP/HTTPS connections are established solely between your Apple TV device and the IPTV server host specified in your configuration. LivingRoom IPTV acts strictly as a local media player and does not proxy, inspect, or log your media traffic.

5. Children's Privacy

Because our application does not collect any personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR.
